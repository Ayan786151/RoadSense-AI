1. PROJECT OVERVIEW & CIVIC IMPACT
================================================================================

RoadSense AI is an end-to-end Kinetic Infrastructure Intelligence and Traffic 
Safety Management System. It bridges real-time computer vision with longitudinal 
predictive machine learning to solve four major urban transportation challenges:

1. High Two-Wheeler Vulnerability & Safety Non-Compliance:
   In fast-growing cities, >70% of fatal traffic accidents involve two-wheelers. 
   Failure to wear helmets, red-light jumping, and dangerous triple-riding are 
   primary contributors. RoadSense AI detects these violations automatically from 
   CCTV feeds and generates legally valid Digital E-Challan citations mapped to 
   the Motor Vehicles Act (MVA) 1988.

2. Inefficient Static Traffic Signals:
   Traditional timers (e.g., fixed 90 seconds green) cause massive congestion on 
   busy approaches while green lights waste time on empty lanes. RoadSense AI 
   calculates Dynamic Traffic Signal Control (DTSC) green durations in real-time 
   (15s to 120s) based on vehicle density, speed, and queue kinematics.

3. Environmental Carbon Emissions & Fuel Waste:
   Vehicles trapped in stop-and-go congestion emit 2.5x more CO2 than free-flowing 
   traffic. RoadSense AI quantifies kilograms of CO2 prevented, liters of fuel 
   saved, and tree-equivalent absorption resulting from signal optimization.

4. Lack of Predictive Long-Term Risk Planning:
   Municipal traffic departments typically react after accidents happen. RoadSense 
   AI trains a leakage-free Supervised ML Risk Forecaster across 50 municipal 
   zones over 52 weeks to predict the probability of an accident occurring in 
   upcoming weeks, enabling proactive police and engineering interventions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Root Entrypoint & Simulator
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• app.py
  - Purpose: The master entrypoint for the Streamlit web application.
  - Features:
    * Implements a custom dark-mode design system with clean typography (Inter & JetBrains Mono).
    * Provides a sidebar navigation menu with 4 main operational modules:
      1. Simulation & Risk Engine
      2. Live CCTV Surveillance
      3. Computer Vision Studio
      4. Geospatial Incident Radar
    * Displays high-level status badges, telemetry summaries, and active session explorers.

• sim.py
  - Purpose: Generates a realistic, 52-week synthetic urban traffic panel dataset 
    across 50 municipal zones (2,600 total observations).
  - How it works:
    * Initializes 50 distinct zones categorized into 6 urban archetypes:
      Commercial Downtown, Residential, University District, Industrial Corridor, 
      Highway Junction, and Suburban Low-Density.
    * Implements 6 distinct temporal dynamics profiles:
      1. STABLE: Predictable weekly baseline.
      2. GRADUAL_DETERIORATION: Worsens progressively (+22.0 drift over 52 weeks).
      3. GRADUAL_IMPROVEMENT: Improves progressively (-16.0 drift over 52 weeks).
      4. PERIODIC_SPIKE: Surges every 5 weeks (e.g., bi-monthly stadium/market traffic).
      5. EVENT_SENSITIVE: Reacts sharply to municipal festivals and conventions.
      6. RECOVERY_AFTER_DISRUPTION: Worsens -> bottlenecks -> remediates -> stabilizes.
    * Models weather shocks (Monsoon in weeks 31-36, Winter storms in weeks 48-52).
    * Simulates non-linear physics: effective capacity degradation, sigmoid congestion 
      response curves, Poisson red-light violations, and Bernoulli incident occurrence.
    * Outputs to `data/traffic_simulation.csv`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.2 Machine Learning & Risk Forecasting (models/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• models/train_model.py
  - Purpose: Trains, validates, compares, and exports the Supervised Machine Learning 
    Risk Model predicting the probability of an incident: P(incident_occurred = 1).
  - Pipeline Design:
    * Chronological data split: Weeks 5-40 (Train: 1,800 rows), Weeks 41-46 (Val: 300 rows), 
      Weeks 47-52 (Test: 300 rows). Weeks 1-4 are dropped as historical warm-up.
    * Preprocessor: scikit-learn ColumnTransformer with StandardScaler for 37 numerical 
      features and OneHotEncoder for 3 categorical features (zone_type, weather, road_condition).
    * Model Comparison: Compares Logistic Regression (baseline) against a Random Forest 
      Classifier (150 trees, max_depth=6, class_weight="balanced").
    * Evaluation Metrics: Computes Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, ROC-AUC, 
      PR-AUC, Brier Score, and Confusion Matrices.
    * Exports the best pipeline to `models/best_risk_model.pkl` and test predictions to 
      `data/risk_predictions.csv`.

• models/MODEL_CARD.md
  - Purpose: Industry-standard documentation disclosing model architecture, intended 
    use, evaluation results, ethical boundaries, and data limitations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.3 Temporal Feature Engineering & Data Audits (analysis/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• analysis/temporal_features.py
  - Purpose: Ingests raw simulation data and constructs 40 leakage-free temporal 
    momentum features required by the ML model.
  - Feature Categories:
    1. Lag-1 Features (Week t-1): Previous-week density, congestion, speed, violations, incidents.
    2. Rolling 4-Week Historical Features (Weeks t-4 to t-1): Rolling averages, cumulative 
       incident counts, and historical incident rates.
    3. Week-over-Week Deltas (Week t vs t-1): Absolute changes in density, congestion, speed.
    4. Percentage Changes: Relative weekly shifts in key traffic metrics.
    5. 4-Week Linear Trend Slopes: Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) slope of congestion, density, 
       speed, and incidents over past 4 weeks.
    * Strict Leakage Prevention: Uses `.shift(1)` so current week t is NEVER included in history.
    * Outputs to `data/simulation_temporal_features.csv`.

• analysis/validate_sessions.py
  - Purpose: Automated test suite auditing CCTV session files, ensuring tracker states, 
    unique vehicle IDs, and metrics remain consistent across multiple video recordings.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.4 Priority & Municipal Intervention Engines (priority/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• priority/priority_engine.py
  - Purpose: Combines ML risk probability with real-world exposure to compute an 
    actionable Municipal Priority Score (0-100) and rank zones 1 to 50.
  - Formula:
    Priority Score = (0.40 * Risk_Score) + 
                     (0.25 * Population_Exposure_Score) + 
                     (0.20 * Vehicle_Exposure_Score) + 
                     (0.15 * Temporal_Trend_Score)
  - Output Classifications:
    * CRITICAL: Score >= 75 (Requires urgent multi-department deployment)
    * HIGH: Score 55 - 74 (Scheduled intervention in current cycle)
    * MODERATE: Score 35 - 54 (Active monitoring and review)
    * LOW: Score < 35 (Routine baseline monitoring)
  - Merges real geographic coordinates from `data/location_mapping.csv` and outputs 
    to `data/final_priority_results.csv`.

• priority/intervention_engine.py
  - Purpose: Evaluates 10 explicit, auditable municipal intervention rules based on 
    current traffic metrics and generates structured, human-readable explanations.
  - Sample Rules Triggered:
    * Traffic Signal Optimization: Triggered when Congestion >= 60 and Speed < 35 km/h.
    * Speed Calming & Enforcement: Triggered when Speed > 55 km/h and Risk > 50.
    * Red-Light Camera Activation: Triggered when Violations >= 8.
    * Pedestrian Crossing Protection: Triggered when Population > 10,000 and Congestion > 50.
    * Road Maintenance / Pothole Repair: Triggered when Road Condition is Poor and Risk > 40.
    * Urgent Safety Inspection: Triggered when 4-Week Trend > 75 and Risk > 60.

4.5 Computer Vision, Tracking & Safety Systems (vision/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• vision/enhancement.py
  - Purpose: Adaptive low-light, nighttime, and glare mitigation preprocessor.
  - Math & Technique:
    * Converts BGR image into CIELAB color space, separating Luminance (L*) from color (a*, b*).
    * Applies Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) with clipLimit=2.5 
      and tileGridSize=(8, 8) to enhance dark vehicle edges without amplifying noise.
    * For deep night scenes (L* < 40), applies a non-linear power-law gamma lookup table 
      (gamma=0.70) to brighten shadows.
    * Daytime frames (L* >= 65) pass through untouched with zero computational distortion.

• vision/calibration.py
  - Purpose: Perspective homography engine mapping 2D camera pixels onto a real-world 
    2D ground coordinate plane in meters.
  - Math & Technique:
    * Uses a 4-point quadrilateral homography matrix H calculated via `cv2.getPerspectiveTransform`.
    * Computes real-world ground coordinates: `s * [X, Y, 1]^T = H * [u, v, 1]^T`.
    * Supports wide-angle lens undistortion using radial/tangential coefficients (k1, k2, p1, p2).

• vision/vehicle_detector.py
  - Purpose: Primary object detection and vehicle tracking pipeline.
  - Features:
    * Uses Ultralytics YOLOv11 neural network for multi-class detection.
    * Universal class resolver: maps classes into 5 categories (cars, 2-wheelers, auto-rickshaws, buses, trucks).
    * Multi-Rider Fusion (`fuse_driver_pillion_riders`): detects vertically stacked rider boxes 
      on a motorcycle and merges them into a single physical 2-wheeler unit to eliminate double-counting.
    * Implements ByteTrack persistent tracking across video frames.

• vision/movement_analyzer.py
  - Purpose: Calculates vehicle kinematic trajectory metrics and true physical speed in km/h.
  - Math:
    * Uses bottom-center of bounding boxes (road-contact point).
    * Projects points through homography H into metric coordinates (X_meters, Y_meters).
    * Computes displacement Delta_d and elapsed time Delta_t -> Speed (km/h) = (Delta_d / Delta_t) * 3.6.
    * Excludes first observation (assigned NaN) and filters out non-physical teleportation jumps.

• vision/kalman_tracker.py
  - Purpose: 2D Constant-Velocity Kalman Filter on the ground plane.
  - Math:
    * State vector: `x = [X, Y, vX, vY]^T` (Position in meters, Velocity in m/s).
    * Continuous-acceleration white noise motion model with process covariance Q(dt).
    * Occlusion gating: When a vehicle is occluded by another vehicle in front, the tracker 
      coasts on momentum rather than allowing corrupted measurements to skew velocity.

• vision/helmet_detector.py
  - Purpose: Real-time safety compliance detection for two-wheeler riders.
  - Mechanism:
    * Extracts rider head region (upper 35% of two-wheeler bounding box).
    * Evaluates head region using fine-tuned YOLO weights or chromatic edge inspection.
    * Classifies as HELMET (compliant) or NO_HELMET (violation) and logs event records.

• vision/red_light_detector.py
  - Purpose: Automated red-light running and virtual stop-line breach enforcement.
  - Mechanism:
    * Analyzes traffic light state (RED / YELLOW / GREEN) using HSV chromatic energy segmentation.
    * Establishes a virtual horizontal stop-line across intersection lanes.
    * Tracks vehicle bottom-center points; if a vehicle crosses the stop line during a RED phase, 
      a RED_LIGHT_RUNNING violation is triggered with timestamp and track ID.

• vision/triple_riding_detector.py
  - Purpose: Detects overloaded two-wheelers carrying >2 persons (Section 128 MVA).
  - Mechanism:
    * Evaluates horizontal bounding box aspect ratio and upper-body contour cluster density.
    * Flags motorcycles where multiple rider silhouettes exceed safe two-passenger capacity.

• vision/violation_tracker.py
  - Purpose: Unified orchestrator running vehicle tracking, helmet detection, and red-light 
    enforcement simultaneously on video files, exporting `violation_events.csv`.

• vision/adaptive_engine.py
  - Purpose: Autonomous scene self-calibration without manual user configuration.
  - Features:
    * Dynamic Stop-Line Discovery: Uses Canny edge detection and Probabilistic Hough Line 
      Transform (`cv2.HoughLinesP`) to find painted road markings, smoothed via Exponential Moving Average.
    * Optical Signal Tracking: Continuously tracks traffic light chromatic shifts.

• vision/feature_fusion.py & traffic_analyzer.py
  - Purpose: Relational database join merging vehicle count density with kinematics metrics 
    on exact timestamps (`timestamp_seconds`), producing combined congestion scores.

• vision/train_*.py (Training scripts)
  - `train_helmet_model.py`: Fine-tunes YOLO for helmet vs no-helmet detection.
  - `train_violation_model.py`: Multi-class safety violation training pipeline.
  - `train_uvh26.py`: Fine-tunes YOLO on IISc AIM Indian traffic datasets (14 vehicle classes).
  - `train_all.py`: Master one-click script that trains all ML and computer vision models in sequence.

4.6 Adaptive Intelligence, CO2 & Legal E-Challan (intelligence/)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

• intelligence/signal_co2.py
  - Purpose: Computes dynamic green-light signal allocation and quantifies environmental savings.
  - Dynamic Signal Control (DTSC):
    * Adjusts archetype base green times (25s-50s) using congestion multipliers (1.0x-1.8x),

  - Purpose: Automated executive briefing generator translating complex metrics into plain English.

    * Deterministic template fallback if no internet/API key is present.

• intelligence/echallan_generator.py
  - Purpose: Converts detected safety violations into legally enforceable Digital E-Challan citations.
  - Features:
    * Generates unique verifiable citation IDs (e.g. `RS-2026-F982A1`).

      - No-Helmet: Section 129 r/w Section 194D (Rs 1,000 fine + 3-month license suspension).
      - Red-Light Jumping: Section 119 r/w Sections 177A & 184 (Rs 5,000 fine).
      - Triple-Riding: Section 128 r/w Section 194C (Rs 1,000 fine).
      - Over-Speeding: Section 112 r/w Section 183 (Rs 2,000 fine).
    * Renders a high-aesthetic, print-ready HTML citation ticket with MD5/SHA-256 verification hash.

5. ALL MODELS EXPLAINED (WHAT EACH MODEL DOES & HOW IT WORKS)
================================================================================

RoadSense AI uses a multi-tiered combination of Machine Learning, Deep Learning, 
Generative AI, and Kinematic Mathematical Models:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Supervised ML Classification Models
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Random Forest Classifier (Primary Model in `models/best_risk_model.pkl`)
  - Role: Predicts weekly incident probability `P(incident_occurred = 1)` for any zone-week.
  - Configuration: 150 decision trees, max depth = 6, min_samples_leaf = 8, class_weight = "balanced".
  - Why it is used: Non-linear ensemble model that handles complex feature interactions 
    between congestion, weather, violations, and 4-week trend slopes without overfitting.
  - Preprocessing: Numerical features normalized with StandardScaler; categorical features 
    encoded with OneHotEncoder.

• Logistic Regression (Baseline Model)
  - Role: Acts as an interpretable linear baseline to validate the performance of the Random Forest.
  - Configuration: L2 regularization (C=0.1), balanced class weights.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.2 Deep Learning Object Detectors
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Ultralytics YOLOv11 (YOLOv11s / YOLOv11n)
  - Role: Real-time object detection across CCTV video frames.
  - Classes Detected: Cars, Motorcycles/Bicycles (2-Wheelers), Auto-Rickshaws, Buses, Trucks.
  - Why it is used: State-of-the-art inference speed (30-60+ FPS on GPU) with high accuracy 
    under diverse real-world lighting and weather conditions.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.3 Specialized Safety & Fine-Tuned Models
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Helmet Violation Model (`models/helmet_yolo.pt`)
  - Role: Detects presence vs absence of protective helmets on 2-wheeler riders.
  - Classes: `helmet`, `no_helmet`, `motorcyclist`.

• Multi-Violation Model (`models/violation_models/`)
  - Role: Dedicated fine-tuned YOLO model for traffic lights (red/yellow/green) and rider infractions.

• IISc AIM UVH-26 / BMD-45 Fine-Tuned Indian Traffic Model
  - Role: 14-class specialized object detector trained on real Indian traffic datasets 
    (handling auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, tempos, and mixed lane discipline).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.4 Algorithmic, Kinematic & Mathematical Models
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 2D Ground-Plane Kalman Filter (`vision/kalman_tracker.py`)
  - Role: Estimates smooth velocity (vX, vY) and filters out camera jitter and occlusion.
  - Model: Constant-velocity state transition with continuous-acceleration process noise Q(dt).

• Planar Perspective Homography Matrix (`vision/calibration.py`)
  - Role: Mathematical 3x3 projective transformation matrix mapping camera pixels to ground meters.

• Hough Line Road Marking Discovery (`vision/adaptive_engine.py`)
  - Role: Canny edge detection + Probabilistic Hough Lines (`cv2.HoughLinesP`) to locate stop lines.

• CIELAB CLAHE + Gamma Correction (`vision/enhancement.py`)

7. KEY MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS & DECISION LOGIC
================================================================================

1. Incident Probability Log-Odds (Simulator):
   risk_logit = -3.40 + 0.036*Congestion + 0.095*Violations + 0.040*max(0, 42 - Speed)
                + 0.350*max(0, Pressure - 1.0) + 0.250*Historical_Memory + Weather_Risk + Road_Risk
   P(incident) = 1 / (1 + e^(-risk_logit))

2. Traffic Pressure & Non-Linear Congestion:
   Traffic Pressure = Vehicle_Density / Effective_Road_Capacity
   Congestion = 100 * Sigmoid(3.4 * (Traffic_Pressure - 0.85)) * Weather_Multiplier

3. Planar Perspective Homography:
   s * [X_world, Y_world, 1]^T = H * [u_pixel, v_pixel, 1]^T
   where H is the 3x3 projective transformation matrix computed from 4 reference points.

4. Kinematic Real-World Speed:
   Delta_d = sqrt((X_t - X_{t-1})^2 + (Y_t - Y_{t-1})^2)  [meters]
   Speed (km/h) = (Delta_d / Delta_t) * 3.6

5. Composite Municipal Priority Score:
   Priority = (0.40 * Risk_Score) + (0.25 * Pop_Exposure) + (0.20 * Veh_Exposure) + (0.15 * Trend_Score)

6. Adaptive Green Phase Duration:
   Recommended_Green = Base_Green * Congestion_Factor * Density_Factor * Speed_Factor * Event_Boost * Weather_Boost
   Clamped between 15 seconds (minimum clearance) and 120 seconds (maximum cycle).

7. Weekly CO2 Savings from Signal Optimization:
   Idle_CO2 = Estimated_Vehicles * Idle_Fraction * 2.3 kg_CO2/hr * Weekly_Hours
   Potential_Savings = Idle_CO2 * (0.20 + 0.10 * (Congestion / 100))

  - Select a recorded session (e.g. `session_001` or `session_002`).
  - Show the Vehicle Kinematics timeline, Vehicle Classification breakdown pie chart, 
    and the Perspective Homography Trajectory scatter plot.

    the print-ready digital citation ticket.
